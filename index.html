Overall

Choose any audio file, drag files in from Files, or record with the mic. Your sounds become part of the ecosystem. Cloud grinds a sound into a granular texture that swells with the wind. Scatter plays it like a creature, and the birds and owls answer. Bed crossfades it into a slow loop that breathes with the world. In key retunes it to the current scale. Planted blooms will also borrow your Scatter sounds.

Presets

Ready-made worlds. Each sets the key, time of day, weather, the mix and which layers are on, and a few plant blooms. Your own sounds and the master volume are left alone.

My scenes

A scene remembers everything: mix, key, time, weather, drift, your sounds’ settings and your blooms. Load brings it back, Update overwrites it with what you have now.

Key

Drift moves the world to a related key every few minutes, gliding the drone across. Changing the key retunes the drone, the creatures, the blooms and any sound set to “In key”. Choosing a key locks it, so “New world” keeps it until you unlock it.
